Services

Integrative care, tailored to you

Herbal Medicine

Botanical medicine has been used for thousands of years - and for good reason. I create custom herbal formulas using high-quality, evidence-informed herbs to support your specific health goals. Whether you need immune support, hormonal balance, digestive relief, or nervous system calm, there's a plant for that.

All formulas are tailored to you and reviewed regularly as your health evolves.

Nutrition Counselling

Food is medicine - but nutrition advice shouldn't feel complicated or impossible to follow. I take a flexible, realistic approach to dietary guidance that fits your life, your culture, and your preferences.

We'll look at how your current eating patterns affect your energy, digestion, mood, and make targeted adjustments that are actually sustainable.

Acupuncture

Acupuncture is one of the most versatile tools in my practice. I use it to address pain, stress, sleep issues, hormonal concerns, digestive complaints, and more. Most patients find it deeply relaxing - many fall asleep on the table!

I practice traditional acupuncture informed by both classical Chinese medicine theory and contemporary research.

Acupuncture is available in person through my clinic. This private practice operates virtually - please contact me for help to book an acupuncture appointment.

Lab Assessment

Lab assessment is a valuable part of naturopathic care. I offer comprehensive panels - including hormone panels, thyroid markers, nutrient levels, inflammatory markers, and more - to build a thorough picture of your health.

I also review existing labs with a functional and holistic lens, looking at optimal ranges alongside standard reference ranges, so we can identify patterns and preventative health opportunities early.

Supplementation

Nutraceuticals - including vitamins, minerals, amino acids, probiotics, and targeted therapeutic supplements - can play a meaningful role in supporting your health goals. When indicated, I recommend evidence-informed supplements as part of a broader treatment plan.

Recommendations are always specific to you: based on your labs, your symptoms, and what you're already taking. I also review existing supplements to make sure everything you're using is appropriate, well-dosed, and working together effectively.
