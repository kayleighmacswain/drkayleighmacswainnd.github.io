Services

Integrative care, tailored to you

Herbal Medicine

Botanical medicine has been used for thousands of years — and for good reason. I create custom herbal formulas using high-quality, evidence-informed herbs to support your specific health goals. Whether you need immune support, hormonal balance, digestive relief, or nervous system calm, there's a plant for that.

All formulas are tailored to you and reviewed regularly as your health evolves.

🥗

Nutrition Counselling

Food is medicine — but nutrition advice shouldn't feel like punishment. I take a flexible, realistic approach to dietary guidance that fits your life, your culture, and your preferences. No extreme elimination diets, no guilt.

We'll look at how your current eating patterns affect your energy, digestion, mood, and inflammation, and make targeted adjustments that are actually sustainable.

🪡

Acupuncture

Acupuncture is one of the most versatile tools in my practice. I use it to address pain, stress, sleep issues, hormonal concerns, digestive complaints, and more. Most patients find it deeply relaxing — many fall asleep on the table.

I practice traditional acupuncture informed by both classical Chinese medicine theory and contemporary research.

🔬

Lab Assessment

Standard bloodwork is a starting point, not the whole story. I offer comprehensive lab panels that go beyond the basics — including hormone panels, thyroid markers, nutrient levels, inflammatory markers, and more.

I also review existing labs with a functional lens, looking at optimal ranges rather than just "normal" ranges, so we catch patterns before they become problems.
