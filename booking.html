New patients welcome

What to expect

Your first visit is 75 minutes — long enough to actually get to know you. We'll go through your full health history, current concerns, lifestyle, and goals. You'll leave with a clear, personalized plan.

Follow-up visits are 45 minutes and are where the real work happens: reviewing your progress, adjusting your plan, and going deeper.

Before your visit

  • You'll receive an intake form by email — please complete it before your appointment.
  • Bring any recent lab results or relevant medical records if you have them.
  • Wear comfortable clothing if you're booking an acupuncture visit.

Insurance & payment

Most extended health benefit plans cover naturopathic visits. I accept payment by credit card, debit, or e-transfer. Receipts are provided for all visits.

Cancellation policy

Please give at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee.
