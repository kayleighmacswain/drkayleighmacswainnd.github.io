What to expect

1

Book Your Appointment

Once you book your initial visit, you'll receive an email with your intake form. Please complete it before your appointment - it helps us make the most of our time together.

2

Initial Visit Investigation

Your first visit is 60–75 minutes. We'll thoroughly investigate your health history to get to the root of your concerns and plan out your goals together. Bring any recent lab results or medical records if you have them.

3

Personalized Support Plan

In your follow-up visit, we'll start your healing journey by discussing your new individualized plan. Follow-up visits are 30, 45, or 60 minutes - we'll check in on your progress, adjust your plan, and do any troubleshooting.

All visits through this practice are conducted virtually. For in-person acupuncture in Toronto, please check my instagram bio for a booking link.

Before your visit

  • You'll receive an intake form by email - please complete it before your appointment.
  • Bring any recent lab results or relevant medical records if you have them.

Insurance & payment

Visit fees are HST exempt & most extended health care plans provide partial to full coverage for naturopathic visits. Touch base with your own insurance provider to find out more.

I accept payment by credit card or e-transfer. Receipts are provided for all visits.

Cancellation policy

Please give at least 48 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations are subject to a fee equal to 50% of your booked visit.
