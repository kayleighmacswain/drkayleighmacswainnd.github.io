About Dr. MacSwain

Naturopathic Doctor · Toronto West End

Dr. MacSwain is passionate about making people feel better through individualized care, a focus on the underlying causes of their concerns, and preventative medicine. ♡

Dr. MacSwain prioritizes holding space for humans from diverse sociocultural backgrounds, genders, and sexual orientations in her practice. She believes that meeting patients where they're at and collaboratively working together are central to achieving their health goals.

Get to know Dr. MacSwain

  • She's from the Maritimes and always missing the ocean a little bit
  • She worked for a farm in Nova Scotia for years and now loves whole foods and gardening
  • She's a big reader and loves new recommendations!

Focus

  • Women's Health (fertility, prenatal, postpartum, and menopause support)
  • Hormonal Balance & Optimization
  • Digestive Health
  • Mental Health Support
  • Preventative & Lifestyle-Based Care

Qualifications

  • Doctor of Naturopathy (ND) - Canadian College of Naturopathic Medicine (CCNM)
  • Registered with the College of Naturopaths of Ontario (CONO)
  • Member, Canadian Association of Naturopathic Doctors (CAND)
