About Dr. MacSwain

Naturopathic Doctor · Toronto West End

My approach to care

I became a naturopathic doctor because I believe people deserve more than a 10-minute appointment and a prescription. Real healing takes time, curiosity, and a willingness to look at the whole picture — not just the symptom in front of you.

My practice is rooted in the naturopathic principle of treating the cause. That means we dig into your history, your lifestyle, your labs, and your goals before we build a plan together. Nothing is one-size-fits-all here.

I work with patients dealing with hormonal imbalances, digestive concerns, chronic fatigue, stress, skin conditions, and more. Whether you're navigating a complex chronic condition or simply want to feel better in your day-to-day life, I'm here for it.

Education & credentials

  • Doctor of Naturopathy (ND) — Canadian College of Naturopathic Medicine (CCNM)
  • Registered with the College of Naturopaths of Ontario (CONO)
  • Member, Canadian Association of Naturopathic Doctors (CAND)
  • Certified in Traditional Chinese Medicine Acupuncture

A little more about me

When I'm not in the clinic, you'll find me at the farmers' market, experimenting with fermentation, or hiking somewhere with too many mosquitoes. I live and practice in Toronto's West End, and I genuinely love this community.
